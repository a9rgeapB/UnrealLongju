# Shared dungeon stages

Each map has its own server process. A dungeon uses one map and one `UWorld` containing its room meshes. Each placed `AMT2DungeonRoom` is a logical stage shared by every player in that stage: mobs, bosses, door state and encounter time are shared, not instanced per party.

`UMT2DungeonSubsystem` keeps world-local weak room, player and enemy mappings. Room IDs must be unique and nonempty within the world. A player/enemy cannot belong to two rooms simultaneously. No additional worlds, listeners or process topology are introduced.

## Authoring and lifecycle

Place one Dungeon Room actor per stage. Give it a unique `RoomId`, fit its `Bounds` to that room's actual meshes, and position its `Entrance` at a safe player spawn point inside those bounds. Bounds must not overlap other stages. Assign placed doors and an optional `NextRoom`. Door actors must have stable level references, or be replicated actors when created dynamically.

The lifecycle is `Idle → Active → Completed/Failed → Idle`. Players enter/leave through overlap events; authoritative quest/Blueprint code may also call `JoinPlayer` after verifying their position. Start the encounter explicitly with `StartEncounter` after gathering participants. Repeated starts cannot duplicate an active encounter.

`Spawns` uses imported mob VNUMs and room-local transforms, including capsule height. A failed spawn fails the encounter and cleans up the partial room-owned batch; it does not silently turn into a completed stage. Existing map regen must not also spawn the same encounter mobs. Alternatively, the authoritative encounter script can spawn mobs and call `RegisterEnemy` while active. Bosses use the same tracking.

Generic rooms complete when all tracked objectives die. Scripted subclasses can disable elimination completion and handle enemy deaths through native lifecycle hooks, so killing a puzzle's support mobs cannot bypass its objective. Destroying a living objective unexpectedly fails the encounter; intentional room purges detach tracking first. Stone summons and resurrection mobs inherit their source's room ownership. Door collision/visibility and shared status are replicated; decorative door animation is not implemented.

The room owns its encounter deadline, independently of player quest timers. `EncounterSeconds=0` disables the deadline for untimed encounters. Late arrivals share an active deadline instead of restarting it. Timeout fails the encounter and opens doors so occupants can leave. An occupied room never resets. Once empty, a configurable grace timer permits cleanup/reset; an admitted returning player cancels that timer. PlayerState and pawn teardown remove membership, and room teardown removes bindings and timers. Room actors have no per-frame Tick.

`AdvancePlayer` only works after completion, stays within the same world, and joins the configured next room after a collision-checked teleport. Invalid destinations fail without transferring ownership; failed membership transfer restores the source position. It does not migrate actors across server processes.

## Individual progress and rewards

Player quest flags, inventory, combat loot and EXP remain individual. Completion eligibility includes persistent character identities present when the encounter starts and players admitted while it is active. Players arriving after completion may share the stage and advance, but do not gain that run's completion entitlement. Leaving and rejoining does not clear an existing claim. This policy is separate from normal combat loot/EXP.

`TryClaimCompletion` consumes at most one entitlement per character per run, while that player is still present in the completed room. It does not itself grant items, EXP or money. Reward delivery must be attached to authoritative quest logic and checked for inventory/save failure; durable exactly-once rewards across process crashes are not implemented here.

`pc.in_dungeon()` now reflects logical room membership instead of always returning false. Other `d.*` quest commands and private-map guards are not automatically translated into working shared-stage behavior.

## Devil Tower integration

The world `/Game/Maps/Game/devil_tower` contains nine authored, non-spatial `AMT2DevilTowerRoom` actors. Bounds come from the actual imported room meshes. Entrances use legacy warp coordinates with the verified 76800-cm X mirror. Arena surfaces are traced through roof/pillar caps and validated for capsule clearance. All three shared room meshes use complex-as-simple collision with regenerated physics data. Fixed legacy points may move a few metres when needed to avoid embedding a Unreal capsule in geometry.

1. `DevilTower.Floor1`: the 8015 entry stone is room-owned. Ambient first-floor groups retain their normal scheduler. Destroying the stone advances all current room members after six seconds.
2. `DevilTower.Floor2`: 205 mobs from `deviltower2_regen.txt`, including group membership and forced aggression. Complete elimination advances current members after four seconds.
3. `DevilTower.Floor3`: 260 mobs from `deviltower3_regen.txt`, including group 1024's 1091 boss. All tracked mobs must die before advancing.
4. `DevilTower.Floor4`: killing 8016 reveals seven 8017 stones, one randomly real. Every ten seconds, fake stones below half health disappear. Killing the real stone completes the puzzle on that update, followed by a five-second transition. The fifteen-minute deadline starts when 8016 dies, with ten/five-minute warnings.
5. `DevilTower.Floor5`: repeating groups from `deviltower5_regen.txt` and five Ancient Seals (20073). Every fifty 1062 kills drops key 50084. Each nearby seal consumes one key. All five must open within twenty minutes; warnings occur every five minutes.
6. `DevilTower.Floor6`: initial groups from `deviltower6_regen.txt`; killing 1092 ends combat and creates one random weapon/armor/accessory blacksmith (20074–20076). Eligible participants receive one material-free refinement attempt. Yang cost, recipe success chance and destruction on failure still apply, as in the legacy money-only refinement path. Item category and refine-set restrictions are enforced. The room waits for an NPC dialogue choice; a level-75 participant can start the six-second group transition. Other choices cancel or leave the tower.
7. `DevilTower.Floor7`: destroy four 8018 stones to activate `deviltower7_regen.txt`. Every 8019 kill drops box 30300. Opening a box has a one-in-ten chance of dropping real map 30302 and stopping regeneration. Using that map completes the stage and starts a six-second transition. A carried map cannot skip the current run's stone puzzle. The real map does not expire on the ground; logging out with it returns a copy to the room before inventory cleanup, preserving shared progression.
8. `DevilTower.Floor8`: repeating `deviltower8_regen.txt` groups. A 1040 kill has a one-in-five key-drop chance; one in ten of those keys is real 30304, otherwise fake 30303. Giving the real key to the owned Sa-Soe seal (20366) completes the stage and starts a six-second transition.
9. `DevilTower.Floor9`: initial `deviltower9_regen.txt` groups. Killing 1093 completes the tower, records individual `deviltower_zone.9_done` for eligible current participants, and schedules departure after sixty seconds. Exits use the existing quest map-transfer path to the Temple, preserving the channel and normal gateway/coordinator routing.

Repeat groups choose among imported weighted alternatives at encounter start and on respawn. A group returns after all its members, including resurrection replacements, die and its source delay expires. Respawn checks run once per second only while the stage needs them. Temporarily blocked respawns retry without committing a partial group. NPC seal props use `AMT2StaticNpc` with the imported static meshes because their legacy models have no skeleton.

Admission starts an idle encounter on the next timer tick, after possession/teleport finishes. The opening stages have no failure deadline, matching the legacy quest. Transition timers belong to the room, not the killer, so one participant's disconnect cannot cancel the group's advance. Collision-blocked transfers leave that player in the source room and log a warning. These rooms have no separate door actors: floor changes use same-world teleportation.

Each new admission sends the current objective to that player's chat history, including late arrivals. Repeated overlap/reconciliation does not repeat it. Completion sends a notice to each current member; arriving after completion shows the completed state. Messages use the reliable owner-client dungeon RPC with `FText`, then chat history, not the info/loot branch. Upper-stage notices are baked from `translate.lua`, retaining legacy wording and `[ENTER]` separators, including the source's floor-number terminology. Opening-stage wording, the initial 8016 instruction and stage-eight completion are additional guidance because those events have no legacy notice.

On this configured map only, quest dispatch bypasses `deviltower_zone`'s old private-map progression and login guard. Its entrance NPC dialogue outside the tower remains available. Other quests are unaffected. Runtime needs only cooked assets, not the legacy source files. Reimporting the map can restore the ambient entry stone; rerun the configuration commandlet afterwards to avoid duplicate stones.

The editor commandlet `-run=MT2ConfigureDevilTower` loads World Partition actors for inspection. Supplying `-LocaleRoot=<server share/locale/italy>` and `-DungeonRoot=<server share/data/dungeon>` validates all rooms, VNUM classes, regen inputs, notices and clear floor positions. `-Apply` authors the two static seal NPC Blueprints from imported models and legacy proto data, rebuilds the VNUM registry, then validates and saves the tower configuration and mesh collision. A failed room preflight does not save room changes, but the NPC import remains saved. Reruns update the same room IDs. `-Verify` checks saved links, initial/puzzle/regen transforms, weighted alternatives, delays, notices, collision policy and ambient stone removal without saving.

`Metin2.Dungeons` covers the shared foundation, opening transitions, puzzle decisions, validated seal/key hand-ins, map replay rejection, blacksmith eligibility and final late-join rewards. Full packaged multi-client progression, navigation, cooking, visual presentation and cross-process exit still require gameplay validation. The native tower does not provide a general private-map `d.*` implementation for other imported dungeon quests.
